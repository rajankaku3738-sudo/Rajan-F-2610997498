1. Taj Mahal (Agra)

An iconic symbol of eternal love, this magnificent white marble mausoleum built by Emperor Shah Jahan is one of the Seven Wonders of the World.

2. Hawa Mahal (Jaipur)

Known as the "Palace of Winds", this unique pink sandstone structure features 953 small windows designed for royal ladies to observe street festivals.

3. South Indian Coastline

Experience pristine tropical beaches with warm waters, lush palm trees, and serene coastal culture.

4. Culinary Heritage Hubs

Explore cultural food centers offering authentic regional delicacies from spicy street eats to royal preparations.
