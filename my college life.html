Academic terms and conditions

Attendance Policy
Students are required to maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each subject to be eligible for examinations.
Grading System
The university follows a letter grading system (A, B, C, D, F) based on the percentage of marks obtained in each subject.

Campus & Administrative Terms

Syllabus
An outline and summary of topics, policies, assignments, grading criteria, and schedule provided by a professor at the beginning of a course.
Office Hours
Designated times set aside by professors and teaching assistants to meet with students individually for extra help or guidance.

Financial Terms

FAFSA
A standardized form filled out annually by students to determine their eligibility for federal financial aid, grants, and loans.
